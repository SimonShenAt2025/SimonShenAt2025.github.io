[Learning Hub](https://learning-hub-nu-opal.vercel.app/) is a course study workspace for learners whose courses are primarily taught in English. It organizes course content by course, section, and lecture.

## Three-step workflow

1. **Add a course URL.** View the course sections and lecture titles in one place.
2. **Export lecture transcripts.** After purchasing the course, use a browser script to retrieve accessible lecture transcripts as structured JSON.
3. **Choose an AI task.** Provide the transcript JSON and a prompt to an AI assistant to summarize, explain, translate, or produce another requested study aid.

The course page also provides a JSON export. What the AI produces depends on the prompt you choose.
