---

I save far more links than I ever read. A bookmark costs nothing to make, so the list only grows.

## The idea

Every saved link gets a 30-day timer. If I write a short note about it before the timer runs out, the link and the note are kept for good. If I don't, the link quietly disappears.

## Why it might work

- **It forces a decision.** Either the link was worth a sentence or it wasn't.
- **The notes are the real value.** A list of notes is something I can search and reuse; a list of URLs isn't.

## Open questions

- Should expired links go to an archive instead of being deleted?
- Is 30 days the right length, or should it depend on the kind of link?
